**Result.**
1. **The strong form of the claim is not supported by S2's own rows.** Several elements the paper treats as gaps do appear in mapped guidelines: named accountability (ACC/AHA row 15; VA/DoD row 23 if valid), bilateral surveillance (GVG row 17), explicit triggers (IWGDF Rec 7, GVG 10.10), destinations and time-bound responses (NICE rows 8–9). Any sentence of the form "no guideline specifies [trigger / destination / response / named owner / bilateral surveillance]" would be contradicted by S2.
2. **VA/DoD 2024 (if row 23 is valid) is the closest single document** in the amputation domain, with accountability, contralateral referral, trigger, destination, response, rehabilitation and an outcome-measure recommendation: about six of nine. It must be read in full before any "most" claim is made. Its transition content was not mapped in S2.
3. **The defensible claim** is narrower: *within the mapped passages, no document links the chain across DFU remission, the revascularised limb and amputation; none specifies confirmation of completed assessment (closed loop); and none defines a shared longitudinal outcome set across those domains.* Even that requires reading each document's full text; S2 selection may have omitted relevant recommendations.
4. **Scope:** the claim can only be made about "the seven mapped documents". Documents not mapped in S2 (for example the IWGDF/ESVS/SVS 2023 PAD-in-diabetes guideline and the other IWGDF 2023 guidelines, ADA Standards of Care foot-care section, regional diabetic-foot guidelines) could integrate more; the manuscript must not state that no guideline does.
5. "Absent" is not declared for any element in this audit; confirmation/closed-loop and shared cross-domain outcomes are the strongest candidates for true gaps, pending full-text checks.
